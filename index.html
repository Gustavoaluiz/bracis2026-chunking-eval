A Neutral and Evidence-Grounded Evaluation of Chunking Strategies in Industrial RAG Systems

Vinícius A. Aguiar, Gustavo L. B. Pereira, Artur M. A. Novais, Thiago Pedroso de Jesus, Sávio S. T. de Oliveira, Anderson S. Soares

Institute of Informatics, Federal University of Goiás (UFG) · Center of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence (CEIA)

PDF (accepted manuscript) Version of Record (Springer)

Abstract

Chunking defines the retrieval units used by retrieval augmented generation (RAG) systems, but evaluations often entangle segmentation with dataset construction. We present an evidence-grounded protocol for evaluating chunking strategies in an industrial RAG setting. The protocol generates questions from full document pages before any chunking step, anchors each question to minimal verbatim evidence snippets, and aligns those snippets to the chunks produced by each method after segmentation. This design avoids favoring the method used to create the supervision. We evaluate token-based, recursive, semantic, LLM-assisted, and adaptive chunking strategies on 165 long-form Brazilian Portuguese documents with over 3M tokens and 887 evidence-anchored questions. Retrieval-only metrics show that structure-aware and LLM-assisted chunking improve evidence coverage over semantic chunking in heterogeneous regulatory and legal corpora. Paired tests with Holm correction at k = 10 support the main recall differences and show no reliable difference between the LLM-assisted strategy and the adaptive decision agent. A secondary check using the RAGAS framework indicates that retrieval gains transfer to contextual precision and relevancy without reducing faithfulness.
